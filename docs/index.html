How the score works

Breakout · 30 pts

Close above the highest high of the prior 20 four-hour bars (about two weeks). Within 2% below it earns partial credit.

Trend · 20 pts

Price above the 50-bar EMA, 20-bar EMA above the 50, and the 50-bar EMA rising over the last 10 bars.

Momentum · 30 pts

RSI(14) between 55 and 72 scores full marks. MACD histogram above zero and rising adds the rest.

Volume · 20 pts

Last bar's volume versus its 20-bar average. 2× or more scores full marks. Over-extended moves (more than 3 ATR above the 20 EMA) lose 10.

Levels

Entry is the last close. Stop is 2 ATR below. Target is 4 ATR above, a 2:1 reward-to-risk.

Tiers

Breakout: confirmed breakout and score 70+. Setting up: 55+. Watch: 40+. A scan during market hours includes the bar still forming, so its volume reads low until it closes.

How it's measured

Date added

The trading day of the scan that first flagged the stock as a breakout. A stock flagged again within 7 days isn't added a second time.

Breakout price

The entry price shown on the board when the stock was first flagged. Scans during market hours use the latest price, not the day's close.

Stop and sell limit

The levels recommended when the stock was flagged: a stop 2 ATR below the breakout price and a sell limit 4 ATR above it, a 2:1 reward-to-risk. Place them together as a bracket (OCO) order.

Holding periods

Each return uses the daily close on the first trading day on or after the breakout date plus 1, 2 or 3 weeks, or 1, 2, 6 or 12 calendar months. Until then the cell shows when it's due.

Versus the S&P 500

Each return is compared with SPY, the S&P 500 ETF, from its close on the breakout date to its close on the same end date. "vs SPY" is the stock's return minus SPY's return, so +3.00% means the breakout did 3% better than the market. Green means the breakout beat the market.

What's left out

Returns are price only: no dividends, fees or taxes, and they assume you held through the period rather than exiting at the stop or sell limit. Prices are adjusted for stock splits and updated after every scan.
